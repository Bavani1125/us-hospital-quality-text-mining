1. YELP REVIEWS AND HOSPITAL QUALITY
   (Most directly relevant — use in 3.1.3, 4.3, and Discussion)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[1] Bardach NS, Asteria-Penaloza R, Boscardin WJ, Dudley RA.
    The relationship between commercial website ratings and
    traditional hospital performance measures in the USA.
    BMJ Quality & Safety. 2013;22(3):194-202.
    PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4074426/
    RELEVANCE: Direct study of Yelp ratings vs HCAHPS —
    found correlation of 0.49. Yelp reviewers observe aspects
    related to patient outcomes. One of the most-cited papers
    on this topic.

[2] Ranard BL, Werner RM, Antanavicius T, et al.
    What can Yelp teach us about measuring hospital quality?
    Health Affairs. 2016;35(4):697-705.
    PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4845957/
    RELEVANCE: Compares Yelp review content to HCAHPS domains.
    Finds Yelp covers HCAHPS domains plus 12 additional domains.
    Argues Yelp complements structured surveys.

[3] Synan LT, Eid MA, Lamb CR, Wong SL.
    Crowd-sourced hospital ratings are correlated with patient
    satisfaction but not surgical safety.
    Surgery. 2021;170(3):764-768.
    URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0039606021003366
    RELEVANCE: Compares Yelp, Google, Facebook vs HCAHPS and
    surgical safety indicators. Yelp most correlated with HCAHPS
    but not with surgical outcomes.

[4] Placona AM, Rathert C.
    Are online patient reviews associated with healthcare outcomes?
    A systematic review.
    Medical Care Research and Review. 2022.
    URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/10775587211014534
    RELEVANCE: Systematic review of 32 studies. Consistent
    positive correlations between online reviews and patient
    experience. Mixed results for clinical quality associations.

[5] Ramasubramanian H, Joshi S, Krishnan R.
    Wisdom of the experts versus opinions of the crowd in hospital
    quality ratings: Analysis of Hospital Compare Star Ratings
    and Google Star Ratings.
    Journal of Medical Internet Research. 2022;24(7):e34030.
    PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9364164/
    RELEVANCE: Google ratings correlated with overall CMS star
    rating but not safety or readmission — supports your argument
    that consumer reviews and institutional ratings capture
    different dimensions.

[6] Chakraborty S, Church EM.
    Social media hospital ratings and HCAHPS survey scores.
    Journal of Health Organization and Management. 2020;34(2):162-172.
    DOI: 10.1108/JHOM-08-2019-0234
    RELEVANCE: Direct correlation between social media ratings and
    HCAHPS scores.

[7] Bui H, Bhooshan S, et al.
    A cross-sectional analysis of Yelp and Google reviews of
    hospitals in the United States.
    Academic Emergency Medicine. 2022.
    PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9960977/
    RELEVANCE: Cross-sectional comparison of Yelp and Google
    ratings with CMS Care Compare ratings for 3000 US hospitals.

2. SENTIMENT ANALYSIS IN HEALTHCARE — GENERAL
   (Use in 3.3, background/literature review)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[8] Zunic A, Corcoran P, Spasic I.
    Sentiment analysis in health and well-being: systematic review.
    JMIR Medical Informatics. 2020;8(1):e16023.
    DOI: 10.2196/16023
    RELEVANCE: Systematic review of sentiment analysis methods
    in healthcare. Establishes the field and justifies use of
    NLP for patient feedback.

[9] Gohil S, Vuik S, Darzi A.
    Sentiment analysis of health care tweets: review of the
    methods used.
    JMIR Public Health and Surveillance. 2018;4(2):e43.
    DOI: 10.2196/publichealth.5789
    RELEVANCE: Reviews methods for sentiment analysis on
    health-related social media content.

[10] Denecke K, Deng Y.
     Sentiment analysis in medical settings: new opportunities
     and challenges.
     Artificial Intelligence in Medicine. 2015;64(1):17-27.
     RELEVANCE: Early but foundational overview of sentiment
     analysis applied to health contexts.

[11] Rahim AIA, Ibrahim MI, Chua SL, Musa KI.
     Hospital Facebook reviews analysis using a machine learning
     sentiment analyzer and quality classifier.
     Healthcare (Basel). 2021;9(12):1679.
     PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8701188/
     RELEVANCE: Machine learning sentiment analysis on social
     media hospital reviews — closely related topic and method.

[12] Sentiment analysis in public health: a systematic review
     of the current state, challenges, and future directions.
     PMC. 2025.
     PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12226299/
     RELEVANCE: Most recent systematic review covering VADER,
     BERT, and other tools for public health sentiment analysis.
     Documents limitations of general-purpose tools in healthcare.

[13] Panchal P, et al.
     Prediction of healthcare quality using sentiment analysis.
     Indian Journal of Science and Technology. 2023.
     URL: https://indjst.org/articles/prediction-of-healthcare-quality-using-sentiment-analysis
     RELEVANCE: Directly applies sentiment analysis to predict
     healthcare quality — very close to your thesis scope.
